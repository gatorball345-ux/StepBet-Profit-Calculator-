StepCat Calculator v253.1 — Finalize Estimate Disqualified Toggle Hotfix
Date: August 25, 2026

BUG REPRODUCED
- In Saved History, open an Estimate and choose Finalize This Estimate.
- Enter a Final Earned / Chips value such as 75.46.
- Turn Disqualified / Lost Entry Fee ON. StepCat forces the field to 0.00 and locks it.
- Previously, turning Disqualified OFF did not restore the value that had been entered. On mobile, re-entering the amount could then produce malformed text such as an appended .00.

FIX
- Before Disqualified is turned ON, the finalize dialog now stores the exact current Final Earned / Chips text.
- While Disqualified is ON, Final Earned remains 0.00 and read-only.
- When Disqualified is turned OFF, the prior value is restored exactly and the field becomes editable again.
- Repeated ON/OFF cycles restore the same value without accumulating extra zeros.
- If the field was blank before turning Disqualified ON, turning it OFF restores a blank field.
- Opening a different Finalize This Estimate dialog clears any stale temporary value and resets Final Earned to editable.
