StepCat Quick Start Guide

Quick Start • Illustrated Guide

StepCat Calculator v253.1 + Workbook v253.3

As of August 31, 2026, Workbook v253.3 is the current recommended workbook with StepCat Calculator v253.1. It keeps the v253.2 formulas and reports while shortening the annual membership record label to Membership Fee.

Use StepCat as a guided calculator, or maintain the Google Sheet directly from any signed-in device. Completed records in the Sheet are cross-device; StepCat local fields and Saved History are not.

1. Choose Entry Mode and Membership Type

Tap Estimate for a projection or Finalized for a completed result. Membership Type stays dimmed until Step 1 is confirmed, then receives a gold edge to show that Step 2 is the current action. Confirm Member or Non-Member; the selected choice remains bright gold while the completed section changes to green. Game Entry then receives the gold current-step edge, and the first required field needing attention receives the same gold cue. When all required Step 3 fields are ready, Review Entry becomes the current action and opens Step 4 before Calculate & Save.

2. Complete Game Entry

Step 3 keeps the familiar labeled inputs while reducing phone scrolling. Type only the values shown on the game platform; no field names, tab characters, or specially formatted text are required. Amount fields such as Gross Pot, Entry Fee, and Final Earned accept no more than two digits after the decimal; extra fractional digits and additional decimal points are not retained.

  • Game Details: Game Name, Start Date, End Date, Payment / Record Type, Notes, and Copied Date Format.
  • Calculation Information: Gross Pot, Entry Fee, Eligible Players, and Total Players.
  • Final Result: Final Earned / Chips and Disqualified status for Finalized entries. Estimates require no final amount.

Use Continue to move to the next Step 3 section and Back to return to the previous one. Your entered values remain in place as you move between sections. Choose Show All Fields whenever you want all Step 3 fields visible together.

Finalized entries: complete Final Result

In Finalized mode, the disqualification control appears immediately before Final Earned / Chips. The status reads Disqualified: No for a normal result and Disqualified: Yes when selected; before Finalized mode is ready, the control remains unavailable. Turning it on sets Final Earned to 0.00 and locks the field until switched off.

Leave Disqualified OFF for a normal result. Turn it ON only when the entry was actually disqualified. While ON, Final Earned is locked at 0.00; turning it OFF restores the value that was there before.

3. Review, Calculate & Save

When the required Step 3 values are ready, choose Review Entry. In Guided Sections, Step 3 collapses and dims as a completed group so Step 4 becomes the clear visual focus. Step 4 presents Entry Mode, Membership Type, Game Details, Calculation Information, Final Result together, including Notes and Copied Date Format before anything is saved. Opening the review does not automatically focus a field or open the mobile keyboard.

Choose Edit beside any review group to return to the exact Step 3 subsection. Choose Continue Editing to close the review without saving and return to Game Entry; the selected Step 3 subsection brightens and reopens.

Opening Review Entry does not save anything. A Saved History record is created only after you choose Calculate & Save.

Calculate, save, or begin a new entry

After reviewing Step 4, choose Calculate & Save to validate the entry and store one result. A successful save keeps the completed fields visible for review during the current session while removing them from unfinished-draft recovery. Use View Saved Result to open the newest history card, Undo Last Save to remove that newest record, or Start New Entry when you are ready to reset the form.

Use Clear Game Entry at the bottom of Step 3 when needed. Restore Cleared Fields appears in the same location after clearing.

4. Review or finalize Saved History

Estimate cards show projected earnings while Accounting Result and ROI remain N/A. Each record receives a permanent non-editable SC-#### Record ID. History cards use green for Profit, amber for Draw, red for Loss or Disqualification, blue for Estimate, and teal for Free Game results. The Result Color Guide beside Saved History writes out the same key, and every card also names its result. Tap Finalize This Estimate when the game ends; StepCat updates that same record and keeps its ID and history position.

5. Copy records into the workbook

The calculator is optional. The Google Sheet can be maintained directly on a phone, tablet, or computer. Enter or paste A–M, leave N–Z untouched, and the workbook calculates its own results and reports.

Only records already added to your Google Sheet are available on another device. Saved History, settings, and unfinished entries remain on the device where they were created.

Tap Copy This Sheet Row, open Game Records, select the first empty cell in column A, and paste. Copy All Sheet Rows transfers all saved A–M records without a header row because row 4 already contains workbook headers.

Paste in A–M only. Never overwrite formula columns N–Z.

6. Use the workbook directly or with StepCat

Use one Google Sheets copy: open Workbook v253.3 from Help & Resources, make your own editable copy if desired, then open that same Sheet from any device signed into the same Google account. Workbook v253.3 is self-contained and can be used with or without StepCat.

The workbook uses the app palette while preserving spreadsheet cues: pale gold/cream for user input, blue-gray for protected formulas, green for Profit, gray for Draw, blue for Estimate, yellow for Free Game, and red for losses or warnings.

7. Understand workbook columns A–Z

Enter or paste records in A–M. Protected formulas calculate N–Z automatically. The workbook has 500 active record positions, normally rows 5–504. On mobile, you may insert a whole worksheet row where a record belongs and enter or paste A–M; the red boundary moves down temporarily. Afterward, delete the same number of unused blank rows immediately above the boundary so it returns to row 505. Never delete the red boundary itself. This direct-entry workflow is the workbook's normal independent operating mode; StepCat simply prepares the same A–M inputs for you. For special rows, use Free Game / Chips or Membership Fee in Payment / Record Type.

8. Read reports and totals

Summary gives exact Profit, Draw, and Disqualified counts, excludes subscriptions from Games by Result, and tracks membership fees separately. Game Comparisons separates completed paid games from Estimates, Free Games, and subscriptions. Best Results now shows meaningful high/low and record extremes for profit, positive ROI, elimination rate, challenge length, player fields, Gross Pot, and Free Game chips; genuine 0% elimination rates are eligible.

9. Customize Settings and Help

Each Quick Explanation header includes a short helper line, followed by Expand All and Collapse All controls. Settings changes save automatically. Restore Defaults replaces saved preferences with StepCat’s original settings, including Haptic Feedback set to Low. It does not delete Saved History, current entry information, or spreadsheet records. Section Display, Starting State, Entry Preferences, Help & Guides, Haptic Feedback, and Help & Feedback are grouped separately. Every setting that needs explanation includes an information button. Informational popups use one large Close button. Default-open settings apply when StepCat starts or refreshes without overriding a drawer the user manually opened or collapsed during the current session. Help also explains that StepCat data is device-local and that the Google Sheet is the cross-device record.

10. Install StepCat or send feedback

Write Feedback is available in the main-page footer, Settings, and Help. The Install StepCat prompt reports whether installation is available. Don’t Show Again hides that prompt on the current browser/device, and Show Installation Prompt in Settings restores it. Tap the information button for Add to Home Screen and shortcut alternatives. Installed-app mode keeps the prompt hidden. Feedback prepares a message for your email app.

11. Move records from an earlier spreadsheet

Copy from A5 through your last actual record row and paste into A5 of Workbook v253.3. For example, if row 198 is your last record, copy A5:M198. You do not need to copy unused yellow rows down to the red boundary. Include any blank rows that occur between existing records so their order is preserved. Existing records do not need to be retyped.

Computer: use Paste special → Values only. Phone or tablet: regular Paste is acceptable when only A–M are copied. Minor appearance differences can be corrected later.

Do not copy N–Z, headers, entire rows, entire sheets, reports, or charts.

Release Note

As of August 31, 2026, Workbook v253.3 is recommended with StepCat Calculator v253.1. It retains the August 11 row/formula safeguards and adds the documented mobile boundary workflow, shorter special record labels, alignment refinements, and expanded Best Results reporting. Earlier corrected workbooks can continue to perform the core calculations, so migration is not mandatory solely because Workbook v253.3 exists.

If your workbook predates the August 11 corrections, shows formula/reference problems, or you want the finalized v253.3 presentation, open Workbook v253.3 and transfer A5:M only through the last actual record row. Unused yellow rows down to the red boundary do not need to be copied. No Carryover is needed merely to update; use Carryover only when all 500 active record positions are actually filled.

Cross-device note: the workbook may be maintained directly through Google Sheets. StepCat itself does not synchronize Saved History or unfinished fields between devices.
