Four-Step WorkflowFollow the same four numbered steps shown on the main page.
  1. Step 1 · Entry Mode: choose Estimate or Finalized.
  2. Step 2 · Membership Type: confirm Member or Non-Member.
  3. Step 3 · Game Entry: complete Game Details and Calculation Information. Finalized entries also use Final Result. Guided Sections provides Continue controls; Show All Fields displays the complete form for scrolling and hides those redundant controls.
  4. Step 4 · Review, Calculate & Save: review all entered values together, edit any subsection if needed, then choose Calculate & Save.
After saving: use Copy This Sheet Row when you are ready to add the completed record to the workbook. Clear Game Entry and Restore Cleared Fields remain secondary form actions within Step 3, not additional workflow steps.

Saved HistoryReview device-local storage, copying, deleting, restoring, and permanent record IDs.

Device-local: unfinished fields, settings, and Saved History remain in this browser and device. They do not transfer automatically to another device or browser profile.

Cross-device records: records added to your Google Sheet are available from any device signed into the same Google account. StepCat Saved History and unfinished entries remain in the browser and device where they were created.

  • SC Record ID: permanently identifies the record and is not a spreadsheet row number.
  • Copy This Sheet Row: copies one exact A–M workbook row.
  • Copy All Sheet Rows: copies every saved record.
  • Delete: removes one card and briefly offers Undo.
  • Clear History: creates a dated history backup before clearing all cards.
  • Restore History: warns before replacing non-empty current history.
  • Restore Cleared Fields: restores only the most recently cleared entry form.

Spreadsheet Apps & Devices

Google Sheets provides the complete StepCat formulas, reports, automatic saving, and access from devices signed into the same Google account. Other spreadsheet apps can hold copied rows for basic recordkeeping, but may not reproduce the StepCat formulas or reports.

Adding Records on Mobile

To add a record between existing records, insert a whole worksheet row above the desired position and enter or paste A–M. The red ACTIVE RECORD BOUNDARY moves down temporarily. When finished, delete the same number of unused blank rows immediately above the red boundary to return it to row 505. Never delete the red boundary itself.

Moving Older Records

Workbook v253.2 is the current recommended copy. It keeps the v253.1 reporting and mobile-workflow refinements while finalizing the remaining row-height consistency fixes. If you are updating from an earlier workbook, open the current v253.2 workbook and transfer A5:M only through the last actual record row into A5 (for example, A5:M198 if row 198 is the last record). Unused yellow rows down to the red boundary do not need to be copied. No Carryover is needed merely to update.

  • Computer: use Paste special → Values only.
  • Phone or tablet: select the destination column-A cell, press and hold, and choose Paste.
  • Do not copy: N–Z, headers, entire rows, entire sheets, reports, or charts.
  • 500-record limit: rows 5–504 are the normal active record area. If inserted rows move the boundary down, remove equivalent unused blank rows immediately above it. When all 500 record positions are actually filled, start a fresh workbook and use Carryover.
Rules, Estimates & TroubleshootingCheck result rules, estimate guidance, and common fixes.

Result Rules

  • Profit: Final Earned is above Entry Fee.
  • Draw: a normal completed result at or below Entry Fee; Net Profit is $0.00.
  • Paid Disqualification: Final Earned is $0.00 and the full Entry Fee is lost. When Gross Pot and Eligible Players are available, StepCat also shows an informational estimate of what might have been earned while eligible.
  • Disqualified Free Game: status is recorded with $0.00 monetary loss.
  • Free Game: Entry Fee must be $0. A non-zero fee is blocked. ROI is N/A.
Estimate Tips
  • Add a new challenge after warm-up week ends, when the full starting player count is available to enter as Total Players.
  • Update Eligible Players as the challenge progresses.
  • Total Players adds context; Eligible Players drives the estimate.
  • A below-fee Estimate remains a projection and does not affect cash totals until finalized.
Common Fixes
  • Locked Game Entry: choose Entry Mode, then confirm Member or Non-Member.
  • Calculation pauses: tap Calculate & Save to highlight the first missing or invalid requirement.
  • End Date: it must be the same as or later than Start Date.
  • Locked Final Earned: turn off Disqualified to edit it.
  • Settings: changes save automatically. Restore Defaults replaces saved preferences with StepCat’s original settings, including Haptic Feedback set to Low. It does not delete Saved History, current entry information, or spreadsheet records.
  • Old cached page: close older StepCat tabs, then reload the hosted StepCat v253.1 page so the new Workbook v253.2 resources can replace the older cache.
Installation Instructions & ShortcutsInstall StepCat and understand what remains available offline.

Offline use: install StepCat while connected. After the initial download, the calculator, Saved History, Settings, Help, and cached guides load from this device. Normal installed use favors the device cache, reducing repeated network use.

Internet still required: installation and updates, Google Sheets, file downloads, feedback email, and other external links. When online, the browser may briefly check for a StepCat update.

Device-local records: Saved History and unfinished entries remain in this browser and device. Clearing the app or site data can remove them, so add completed records to the Google Sheet for durable cross-device storage.

Android Chrome: open the three-dot menu and choose Install app or Add to Home screen.

iPhone/iPad Safari: choose Share, then Add to Home Screen.

Computers: Chrome or Edge may show Install app. StepCat also works directly in the browser.

Device note: Saved History and unfinished fields stay in this browser. Use the Google Sheet for records you need on other devices.

Copy one record

  1. Tap Copy This Sheet Row on the saved card.
  2. Open Game Records in Workbook v253.2.
  3. For a new record at the end, select the first empty cell in column A, such as A5, and paste.
  4. For a forgotten game, insert one whole row above the correct Game Records position, then enter or paste A–M. N–Z calculate automatically. The red boundary moves down temporarily; afterward, delete the same number of unused blank rows immediately above it to return the boundary to row 505.

Recommended: tap or click the column-A cell once so the entire cell is selected. Do not double-tap or place the text cursor inside the cell. Enter or paste columns A–M only; the protected N–Z calculations fill automatically. Selecting the row number may work, but selecting the column-A cell is recommended.

How to paste records

  • Phone or tablet: tap the column-A cell once, then press and hold and choose Paste. Keep the text cursor out of the cell.
  • Windows or Chromebook: click the column-A cell once, then use Ctrl+V. Ctrl+Shift+V pastes values only. Right-click or use a two-finger touchpad click for the Paste menu.
  • Mac: click the column-A cell once, then use Command+V. Command+Shift+V pastes values only. Use a two-finger click or Control-click for the Paste menu.
Protect the formulas: paste or edit in A–M only. Never clear or overwrite N–Z, because those columns calculate automatically.

Copy more than one record

Use Copy All Sheet Rows in Saved History, then paste the block beginning in the first empty column-A cell.

Special Cases & Column Notes

Workbook totals: Game Records shows Running Net Game Profit, Gross Profit Earned, and Total Lost Entry Fees at the top. Summary shows Actual Running Net Profit after membership fees. Column Z tracks Running Net Profit row by row.

Notes: optional Notes copy into column M. Hybrid cash-and-points details belong there.

Below-fee Estimates: the exact projected amount appears in formula column O; Final Earned remains blank.

Free Games: in the workbook choose Free Game / Chips. When estimate inputs are unavailable, columns N and O display N/A. ROI remains N/A; recorded chips appear separately and never increase cash-profit totals.

Disqualified rows: Final Earned copies as $0.00 and Disqualified / Lost Entry Fee copies as Yes. Paid entries lose the full Entry Fee; Free Games record no monetary loss.

Annual membership: in the workbook choose Subscription / Membership Fee and enter the annual cost in Entry Fee. Subscription rows are non-game expenses.

Restore History: restores only the most recently cleared Saved History backup. Restore Cleared Fields is separate and affects only the entry form.

You do not have to migrate solely because Workbook v253.2 exists. Earlier corrected workbooks can continue to calculate, but v253.2 is the current finalized public workbook. You do not need to select unused yellow rows down to the red boundary. If blank rows occur between existing records, include them in the selected range so the record order is preserved.
